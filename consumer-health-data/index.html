Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy applies to residents of Washington, Nevada and Connecticut and describes how CueBody LLC ("CueBody") collects, uses and shares "consumer health data" as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada SB 370 and Connecticut law. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. Consumer Health Data We Collect

  • Symptoms, health conditions, diagnoses you tell us about, and health history;
  • Medications and treatments you mention;
  • Reproductive and sexual health information, including menstrual cycles, fertility, pregnancy, contraception and menopause;
  • Mental health and mood information you choose to share;
  • Body measurements and biometric or physiological data from connected wearables (such as heart rate, sleep and temperature);
  • Lab results, medical records and other documents you upload;
  • Inferences we derive from the above, including AI-generated summaries of your health information;
  • The fact that you have signed up for or use CueBody, a women's health service.

[Counsel to verify against the full statutory definitions.]

2. Sources

We collect consumer health data directly from you (through conversations, forms and uploads) and, if you choose, from wearables or health platforms you connect.

3. How We Use Consumer Health Data

  • To provide CueBody's AI health guide, including answering your questions and helping you prepare for appointments;
  • To remember your history and personalize responses;
  • To keep the service safe, including detecting emergencies and reviewing reported or flagged responses;
  • To periodically review de-identified samples of conversations for quality and safety;
  • To comply with law.

We collect and use consumer health data only with your consent, given separately from your agreement to our Terms of Service, or as necessary to provide the service you request. We do not use consumer health data to train AI models or for advertising.

4. Who We Share Consumer Health Data With

We share consumer health data only with service providers (processors) that process it on our behalf under contract:

  • Anthropic, PBC — AI model provider, to generate responses;
  • OpenAI, L.L.C. — AI model provider, to generate responses;
  • [CLOUD PROVIDER] — secure hosting and storage.

We do not share consumer health data with affiliates or with any other third party, except with your separate consent or as legally required (see "Government and Law Enforcement Requests" in our Privacy Policy).

5. We Do Not Sell Consumer Health Data

CueBody does not sell consumer health data. We would never do so without your signed, valid authorization as required by law. We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities and do not collect precise geolocation.

6. Your Rights

  • Confirm and access: learn whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, get a copy, and get a list of all third parties and affiliates with which we have shared it (with their contact information);
  • Withdraw consent to our collection or sharing of your consumer health data;
  • Delete your consumer health data. Deletion requests are passed on to our processors, and data is purged from backups within 6 months;
  • Appeal a decision on your request.

How to make a request: use the privacy settings in the CueBody app, or email legal@cuebody.com. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days. We may extend this by another 45 days when reasonably necessary, and will tell you if we do. Requests are free up to twice in any 12-month period.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you may appeal by emailing legal@cuebody.com with the subject "Appeal." We will respond within 45 days with an explanation. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, the Nevada Attorney General, or the Connecticut Attorney General.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

7. Changes

Before we collect new categories of consumer health data, use it for new purposes, or share it with new categories of recipients, we will update this policy and ask for your consent.
