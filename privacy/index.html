CueBody LLC ("CueBody", "we", "us", or "our") makes an AI health guide for women. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have, when you use the CueBody website, waitlist, apps and related services (the "Services").

If you live in Washington, Nevada or Connecticut, please also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, which describes your additional rights over health data.

1. CueBody and HIPAA

CueBody is a direct-to-consumer service. We are not a HIPAA "covered entity" or "business associate," and the federal HIPAA law does not apply to the information you share with us. Instead, your information is protected by the commitments in this policy, by our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, and by applicable state law, including the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act as it applies to reproductive and sexual health apps.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us.

  • Account and waitlist information: your name, email address, date of birth (to confirm you are 18 or older), and your communication preferences.
  • Conversations: everything you type or say to CueBody, including symptoms, health history, medications, questions and the AI's responses.
  • Cycle and reproductive health information: periods, fertility, pregnancy, contraception, menopause and sexual health information you choose to share.
  • Uploaded documents: lab results, medical records, photos or other files you choose to upload.
  • Feedback and support requests: messages you send us, including reports about AI responses.

Information from connected services. If you choose to connect a wearable or health platform (such as Apple Health or a fitness tracker), we receive the categories of data you authorize, such as cycle, sleep, heart rate or activity data. You can disconnect at any time in settings.

Information collected automatically. We collect limited technical information needed to run and secure the Services, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address (used for security and to confirm you are in the United States, then not stored with your health data), and crash logs. We use privacy-first analytics that do not use advertising cookies, do not build advertising profiles and do not track you across other websites. We do not collect precise geolocation.

Inferences. Information we derive from what you share (for example, that you may be experiencing perimenopause symptoms) is treated as health information and receives the same protections.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Services, including generating AI responses to your questions and helping you prepare for medical appointments;
  • To personalize your experience, such as remembering your history so you don't have to repeat it;
  • To keep the Services safe, including detecting emergencies, preventing misuse and investigating reports of harmful responses;
  • To review the quality and safety of AI responses (see "Human review" below);
  • To communicate with you about your account, the waitlist, and changes to the Services;
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We collect and use health information only with your consent and only for these purposes. If we ever want to use your information for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.

4. How We Use AI

AI providers. CueBody's AI is powered by large language models from Anthropic and OpenAI, which we use for different tasks. When you chat with CueBody, the relevant parts of your conversation are sent to these providers to generate a response. They act as our service providers under commercial agreements: they may process your information only to provide services to us, and they do not use it to train their models. These providers may temporarily retain data (typically up to 30 days) to detect abuse, unless a shorter retention arrangement applies. No training on your conversations. CueBody does not use your conversations, health information or uploaded documents to train, fine-tune or otherwise build AI models — ours or anyone else's.

Minimizing what we send. We remove direct identifiers such as your name and email address from requests sent to AI providers, and we store health information separately from your account identity. [Confirm implemented before publishing.]

Human review. To make sure CueBody is accurate and safe, a small number of authorized CueBody team members periodically review samples of conversations. Conversations are de-identified before review wherever possible. We may also review a specific conversation if you report a response, if our systems flag a safety concern, or if required by law. Reviewers are bound by confidentiality obligations, and access is logged. Reviews are used to improve CueBody's instructions and safeguards, not to train AI models.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We do not upload your email address or other information to advertising platforms. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run the Services — cloud hosting, AI model providers (Anthropic and OpenAI), email delivery, privacy-first analytics and customer support — under contracts that restrict their use of your information to providing services to us. See our list of service providers.
  • At your direction, for example if you choose to export your data or share a summary with your clinician.
  • Legal requests, only as described in "Government and Law Enforcement Requests" below.
  • Business transfers. If CueBody is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different policy, and you will be able to delete your data first.

6. Government and Law Enforcement Requests

We know how sensitive reproductive and sexual health information is. Our commitments:

  • We disclose information only in response to valid, legally binding process. We require a search warrant before disclosing the content of your conversations or health information.
  • We review every request, and we challenge requests that are overbroad, legally deficient, or that seek information about lawful reproductive or gender-affirming care, including where state law (such as California's) prohibits cooperation.
  • We disclose the minimum information required.
  • We notify you before disclosing your information, unless we are legally prohibited from doing so or there is an emergency involving risk of death or serious injury.
  • We will publish a transparency report on the requests we receive. [Confirm cadence.]

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, so CueBody can remember your history. You are in control:

  • You can delete individual conversations or uploaded documents at any time.
  • You can delete your account at any time, which deletes your conversations, health information and uploads.
  • If your account is inactive for 2 years, we will email you and then delete your account and data if you don't respond.
  • Deleted data is removed from our live systems within [30] days and from backups within 6 months. We also instruct our service providers to delete it.
  • We may keep limited records (such as a record that you made a privacy request) where required by law.

Waitlist information is kept until you join CueBody, unsubscribe, or ask us to delete it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Wherever you live in the United States, you can:

  • Access the information we hold about you and get a copy in a portable format;
  • Correct inaccurate information;
  • Delete your information or your account;
  • Withdraw consent to our processing of your health information (this means we can no longer provide the Services to you);
  • Disconnect any connected wearable or health service;
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email;
  • Appeal if we decline your request.

You can exercise most of these rights in the app's settings, or by emailing legal@cuebody.com. We will respond within 45 days. We will never discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, separation of health information from account identity, role-based access controls with logging, and regular security reviews. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affecting your health information occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

11. Age Requirement

CueBody is only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

12. United States Only

CueBody is offered only to people located in the United States. We do not offer the Services in the European Union, the United Kingdom or other countries, and your information is stored and processed in the United States.

13. If CueBody Shuts Down

If we ever discontinue the Services, we will give you advance notice and a window to export your data, and then delete it.
